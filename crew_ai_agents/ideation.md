# Steps to create an Agent that can automate 
## Decide on a topic
Human gives various ideas ans topics that are precise
## Gather Sources 
How would I do this manually? Google it, ask llm , pick up core ideas, google them, build on the resources found. 
## What matters
Out of all the data gathered decide what really matters? How to decide what matters?  Easy to read, simple logic, less coginitive load, factual. 
## Adhere to a writing style 
### Strucutre
- Opening Statement that gives a gist of whats happening
- Any terms used that require explaination?  
- Opening Para with claims/opinions
- Facts supporting claims/opinions
- Examples/Case Studies supporting  claims/opinions
- Closing statement
- Sources 
- hashtags

### Tone 
- Simply stick to the points that matter
- Sassy not humorous 

## Visual/Data/carousels 
- Data visuals if data involved as a major fact supporting the claim
- If data not involved which should always be involved, but if claims supported by a reputed journal, study, statistics. Then an Image representing whats happening. 

## What not to do 
- Do not use emojis
- Do not use sentence formation patterns like 
  **Do not use punchy lines and hooks**
  **X is not great. But Neither is Y**
  **Since X. That's why Y types of logics**
  **Weirdly Optimistic Sentences**
  **Weirdly  imparting knowledge/advices instead of information** 
- Avoid meta commentary and signposting **“Let’s explore…”, “Here’s the truth…”, “The real issue is…”, “Here’s what I found out”**
- Do not use words like 
    **Quiet, Interresting, Exciting, explore**

- Avoid writing one liners. Write in full sentences.
